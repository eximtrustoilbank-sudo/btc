📄 Bitcoin Whitepaper

The official whitepaper of Bitcoin (BTC) — Bridge to Core on Solana

Abstract

Bitcoin (BTC) is a token on the Solana blockchain that serves as a Bridge to Core (BTC) — connecting the legacy of Bitcoin with the speed and scalability of Solana. With a total supply of 2.1 billion tokens (burn to 21 million), BTC brings the king of crypto to the world of DeFi.

1. Introduction

Bitcoin is the most recognized cryptocurrency in the world. However, its limited programmability and high transaction fees have kept it from fully participating in the DeFi ecosystem. Bitcoin (BTC) on Solana solves this by providing a bridge that allows BTC holders to access DeFi applications while retaining the value and trust of Bitcoin.

Key Insight: BTC on Solana is not a wrapped token. It is a native Solana token with a fixed supply of 21 million (after burn), representing the core of crypto on the fastest blockchain.

2. Tokenomics

The tokenomics of BTC are designed to create scarcity and value appreciation:

  • Total Supply (Start): 2,100,000,000 (2.1 billion)
  • Final Supply (After Burn): 21,000,000 (21 million)
  • Transfer Fee: 0.25% (goes to insurance fund and burn)
  • Distribution: 30% Liquidity, 25% Bridge Reserve, 20% Airdrop, 10% Insurance Fund, 10% Team, 5% Burn

3. Bridge to Core

The BTC Bridge allows users to move their Bitcoin (or BTC tokens) between Solana and other networks. This bridge is secured by a decentralized network of validators and smart contracts, ensuring fast and secure transfers.

4. Ecosystem

BTC is integrated with the leading DeFi protocols on Solana, including Jupiter, Raydium, Orca, and Meteora. Holders can trade, stake, and participate in governance using their BTC tokens.

5. Roadmap

  • Phase 1 (Q3 2026): Token launch, liquidity pools, bridge integration
  • Phase 2 (Q4 2026): Staking launch, DAO governance, burn event
  • Phase 3 (Q1 2027): Cross-chain expansion, NFT integration, ecosystem grants

6. Conclusion

Bitcoin (BTC) on Solana represents a new era for the king of crypto. By bridging Bitcoin's legacy with Solana's speed, BTC unlocks the full potential of DeFi for Bitcoin holders worldwide.
